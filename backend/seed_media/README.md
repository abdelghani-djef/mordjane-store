Photos for `seed` and `sync-catalog`, copied into `MEDIA_DIR` at the same relative path.

    products/<slug>.webp            the product's listing photo
    products/<slug>/<grams>.webp    one photo per size (e.g. products/creme-noisettes/200.webp)
    categories/<slug>.webp          optional category photo

The product photos are the supplier's (CEBON, cebon.dz) 1024 px packshots, converted to WebP.
Without a photo the storefront draws an illustration instead.
